# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Stormward collects nothing about you, except the sales you share if
you turn on SHARED PRICES.

- The game plays offline. It goes online only for the things named
  below. Nothing else you do in it leaves your iPad unless you send it
  yourself.
- It has no account of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Saves, settings and save pictures stay on the iPad, in the game's own
  storage. Delete the game and they go with it.
- iCloud saves back up your run slots to your own iCloud account, so
  your other iPad can copy a run. They are on when the iPad is signed
  in to iCloud. Apple holds the copies under your account, and I cannot
  see them. Turn them off under iCloud saves in the game's SETTINGS.
  Copies already in iCloud are yours to delete in iPadOS Settings.
- Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to it, your
  achievements and leaderboard scores go to Apple, and other players
  see them beside your Game Center name. Sign out of Game Center in
  iPadOS Settings to stop that.
- SHARED PRICES is optional and off by default. Turn it on under
  Shared prices in the game's SETTINGS, and each sale you make goes to
  the game's public database in Apple's iCloud: the colony, the good,
  the count, the time and a random id for your copy of the game. Apple
  tags each sale with an id for your iCloud account, so your sales are
  linked to that account. Other players' games read them to move their
  own prices, and I can see them too. The game uses them only for
  prices, not for tracking or ads. Turn it off in SETTINGS to stop
  sending sales.
- Tips are optional and unlock nothing in play. Apple takes the
  payment. I never see your name, card or address: Apple sends me sales
  totals, not who paid. Other players see a supporter badge by your
  name only if you switch that on.
- Where the game uses Apple Intelligence, it runs Apple's on-device
  model. Your questions and its answers stay on the iPad, and the game
  does not store them.
- The bug key opens your own Mail app with a new message to me. It
  holds a picture of the screen and a few lines about the game: build,
  iPad model, iPadOS version, the site you were on and your save slot.
  It also attaches diag.json, a small file about how the game ran: frame
  rate, heat, memory, battery, load times, recent errors, your game
  settings and each payday's figures. It holds no names and nothing you
  typed. You see all of it before you send, and nothing goes unless you
  press Send. I use it only to fix the bug.
- The help link on the game's overview page opens this site in your
  browser. The site is on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs
  under GitHub's privacy policy. I do not see who visits.
- Test builds from TestFlight download one small settings file from
  this site when they start, so I can adjust a test without a new
  build. GitHub sees that request as it sees any visit. The App Store
  game does not download it.
- If you allow it in iPadOS settings, Apple may collect crash reports
  and usage data. That is Apple's collection, under Apple's privacy
  policy. I only see the anonymous crash reports Apple passes on.

If this ever changes, this page changes first, and the App Store
privacy label with it.
