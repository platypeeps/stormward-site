# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Stormward collects nothing about you.

- It makes no network connections. Nothing you do in it leaves your iPad.
- It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Saves, settings and save pictures stay on the iPad, in the game's own
  storage. Delete the game and they go with it.
- Where the game uses Apple Intelligence, it runs Apple's on-device
  model. Your questions and its answers stay on the iPad, and the game
  does not store them.
- If you allow it in iPadOS settings, Apple may collect crash reports
  and usage data. That is Apple's collection, under Apple's privacy
  policy. I only see the anonymous crash reports Apple passes on.

If this ever changes, this page changes first, and the App Store
privacy label with it.
