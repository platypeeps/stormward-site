# Changes

<p class="storm">TEST BUILDS · NEWEST FIRST</p>

Stormward is not out yet. Testers get several builds a day, so this
page groups them by day. The first public release starts its own
section here.

## 22 November

- SPLIT and COMBINE belt tiles. Blueprints keep them.
- WALK IN from THE SHIP panel to see the ship's interior.
- Quarters and a mess hall on the ship. Crew morale goes up and down,
  and it changes aim and refiner speed.
- A town clinic heals every wound at once, for credits.
- Rest aboard the landed ship: the clock runs on and wounds heal
  faster.
- Stand on a landmark to survey that tile and the ones around it.
- Rivals now make moves, and you can counter each one. Watch out for
  grifters at FULL depth.
- A hold-out can repair a building a rival disabled.
- Drag a module onto a slot to make and fit it.
- The build bar has a RECENT row and a COPY tool. Rooms snap to the
  command module.
- Fixed from your reports: CLEAR sweeps from bare ground, the landing
  pad goes anywhere in the yard, a lab snaps beside the module, belts
  start beside any refiner tile, and some labels no longer overlap.

## 21 November

- Board the ship by tapping it. It has room slots, flies with the
  crew and lands where you send it. The hull is now 20 m.
- Five towns on the home map, each with a tavern and a notice board,
  and story characters to meet.
- Bases go anywhere. Raids now roam, with an ATTACKS setting.
- A landmark, a cave and a lair on every tile. Tile edges are open,
  so you can walk straight across.
- Three rivals you can switch on, in a new WORLD tab in SETTINGS.
- Rumours at the boards that lead to missions, and loyalty missions
  for your crew.
- Wounds stay until the crew rests at a base.
- A storm redraws the site and leaves a hostile camp. Storm fights
  run on a turn clock.
- A full-screen map with SITE, WORLD and STARS views, tags you can
  set, and a line for every route.
- An INFO card for every module, and more Game Center achievements.
- Saves from before this day no longer load. Their files stay put.

## 20 November

- Game Center, tips and a Supporter badge.
- Fights show what each enemy plans to do, and give you help with
  aim. Fallen crew get a memorial.
- A debrief shows what was seized when a crime is found.
- Fixed: a storm came after the Storm Engine was stopped.

## 19 November

- New models, drill tiers and art in the codex.
- A new start site, and astronauts that walk better.
- Your runs back up to iCloud.
- Drag to clear what you built. The game asks first.
- An outpost pays for builds with parts when refined stock is short.

## 18 November

- SETTINGS has tabs: GAME, SOUND, LOOK, CONTROLS, ALERTS and ABOUT.
- New settings: a battery saver, FAST fights, read-aloud speed and a
  default zoom.
- REDUCE MOTION follows the iPad setting. Map marks differ by shape as
  well as colour.
- Tutorial pointers, rewards, tips and a guide.
- Tap to place a build. Blocked cells show, and a drag shows a preview.

## 17 November

- Paydays come each day, and the Company buys back what you made.
- Sites have landscape areas. The first storm now comes later.
- Sound settings, and a diagnostics pack to send with a bug report.

## 16 November

- Climate: a dirty generator, a clean solar array, a wind turbine, and
  fission and fusion reactors. Every tonne joins a world total.
- A full fuel cycle: mine uranium, enrich fuel rods, seal spent fuel in
  casks. Fusion burns helium-3.
- The ending: analyse every vault sample, and the Storm Engine shows on
  the map. Reach it to stop it.
- A landing pad, the one door to TRADE.
- Tariffs, taxes and a carbon price in each colony. You can run cargo
  past customs, at a risk.
- Music for each place, with a MUSIC row in SETTINGS, and a hum for
  each kind of machine.
- A radar, a zoomable MAP, and artifacts to find, study and sell.
- Save a base as a blueprint and build from it.
- A DEPTH row in SETTINGS, saved with the world.
- An optional test at the start and after the last vault, a payday
  forecast, and a short debrief after a lost fight or freight.
- The crew shelters in the command module and the buildings linked to
  it.
- Research needs a lab in power reach; modules are made at a powered
  fabricator.
- The build keys sit in categories. Drag a hire's portrait onto a
  machine to staff it.
- Fixed: READ ALOUD made no sound with the iPad muted.

- Sites are ten times wider, with new ground, landmarks on the slopes
  and models for every machine, belt, creature and the ship.
- Drills by tier and node hardness, with modules you fit by tapping.
- Every machine runs by recipe and opens a detail panel.
- Furnace, fabricator and alloy; research tiers that open machines;
  tag triples and precision parts.
- A vault with a bunker gate, blast door and consoles.
- A command module starts the base and taps power.

## 15 November

- A start page, bug reports by mail, and deleting a save.
- Animated astronauts, mountain terrain and weather.
- Debt with interest; each sale pays a share.
- Pinch and scroll zoom, kept between launches.
- Storm timing and enemy difficulty in SETTINGS.
- Two taps to shoot or move in a fight.
- A key to clear what you built.

## 14 November

- The factory: drills, belts, bridges, power and nodes that run dry.
- Walls, turrets, storage and the storm.
- Vaults with four chapters and samples.
- Research, modules with tags, and a screen to compare kits.
- Crew with roles, levels and pay; hired freight, your own ship and
  escorts.
- A 16 by 16 world under fog, bases that run while you are away, and
  routes between them.
- Ten save slots, a megacity look, TEXT SIZE and READ ALOUD.
- The game got its name.

## 13 November

- The first builds: walking a site, the camera, and turn fights with
  cover, sight and overwatch.
